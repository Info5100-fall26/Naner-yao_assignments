## What is “digital twin” of enterprise or an organization?
- A dynamic virtual copy of an organization’s assets, operations, and processes, continuously updated with real-time data so it can be used to simulate, monitor, and analyze the real thing and improve decisions.
## What are business processes?
- A series of coordinated, repeatable tasks that an organization performs to achieve a specific goal and deliver value to customers.
## What are major challenges in designing business processes?
- Poor processes cause inefficiency and losses, processes must be designed as one whole system rather than disconnected pieces, and they are written in ordinary language by leaders who often think they are experts.
## ow to define the problem to be solved by the application?
- Clarify the purpose and scope, separate the core issue from its symptoms, capture stakeholder needs, and document the current state.
## What are system requirements and why they are needed?
- They describe what the system must fulfill, split into functional requirements, which say what it does, and non-functional requirements, which say how well it does it. They are needed because they turn stakeholder needs into a technical view, form the basis for design and verification, and help the team communicate.
## Describe and explain high-level design components.
- They are data flow diagrams, interface design, system architecture, modules and components, the technology stack, and deployment architecture. Together they show the system’s overall structure and how parts interact, without implementation details.
## How to identify initial objects?
- Find the nouns in the problem description or requirements, and consider real-world entities such as customer, product, or order. These become the candidate objects.
## What is the difference between high-level and low-level designs?
- High-level design is the overall architecture and component interaction, including trade-offs between technologies. Low-level design is the detailed implementation plan, covering classes, methods, data structures, and algorithms.
## Describe the types of application testing.
- Manual, automated, functional, performance, security, and regression testing, plus collecting user feedback.
## What is the difference between alpha and beta releases?
- Alpha is an early internal test by developers and QA on an incomplete product. Beta is a later test by external users on a feature-complete product to get real-world feedback before launch.
## What is the difference between application release and deployment?
- Deployment is the technical step of putting code into production. Release is the business decision to make it available to end users.
