## 1 What is SDLC and what are its steps?

SDLC (Systems Development Life Cycle) is the traditional methodology used to develop, maintain, and replace information systems. Its 7 phases are:

- Planning: Identify, justify, and prioritize system needs.
- Analysis: Study and structure requirements (produces the SRS).
- Design: Convert requirements into logical and physical specifications.
- Implementation: Code and test the application.
- Deployment: Install, activate, and support the application.
- Maintenance: Repair and improve the application.
- Obsolescence: Replace the system when it is no longer maintainable.
## List and describe different SDLC types.

- Standard view: The phases proceed in order, from Planning to Obsolescence.
- Evolutionary view: SDLC is a repeating spiral. A new cycle starts when the previous system becomes obsolete.
- Maintenance types: Corrective (fix bugs), Adaptive (new environments), Perfective (improve performance or add features), Preventive (avoid future problems).
- Deployment strategies: Big Bang, Rolling, Blue-Green, Canary, Feature Deployment.
